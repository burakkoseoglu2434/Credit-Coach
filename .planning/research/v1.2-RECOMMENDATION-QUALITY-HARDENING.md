# v1.2 Research Seed: Recommendation Quality Hardening

**Created:** 2026-03-15
**Purpose:** Normalize the new milestone intent before phase planning begins.

## Why This Milestone Exists

`v1.1` hardened fairness, KPI, telemetry, and release evidence contracts. The next dominant gap is no longer measurement stability; it is recommendation realism. The system needs to move from gate-aware offline recourse optimization toward behavior-aware sequential planning that better matches how people and institutions actually act.

## Locked Direction

- Expand the action space beyond raw feature edits into behavioral, structural, and institutional actions.
- Strengthen feasibility constraints so recommendations respect financial capacity and realistic transition limits.
- Model whether users are likely to execute actions, not just whether actions would improve model outputs.
- Evolve one-shot plans into sequential trajectories with partial execution and time-aware risk simulation.
- Treat institution-side offers as candidate actions with their own acceptance and recovery trade-offs.
- Close the learning loop through richer decision logging, outcome ingestion, and off-policy evaluation.

## Guardrails Carried Forward From v1.1

- Fairness, reliability, and telemetry hardening remain non-negotiable inherited gates.
- Recommendation realism may improve lift, but it must not relax governance controls.
- Machine-readable evidence remains canonical; markdown reviews stay derived.
- Brownfield compatibility matters: new planning logic should reuse the existing batch, suite, challenger, and storage seams where practical.

## Open Questions For Phase Context

- Which action classes deserve first-class planner representation versus companion diagnostics?
- How conservative should execution-probability assumptions be before they influence ranking?
- Which recovery proxies should offer optimization prioritize under fairness and reliability constraints?
- What minimum outcome coverage is required before off-policy learning artifacts become promotion evidence instead of diagnostics?
