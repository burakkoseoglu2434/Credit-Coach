# Milestone v1.3: Portfolio Intelligence & Productization

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-03-20
**Phases:** 17-25
**Total Plans:** 28

## Overview

This archive captures the milestone that turned Credit Coach from a recommendation-quality engine with partial website surfaces into a portfolio-proven, buyer-safe, analyst-safe, and pilot-baseline product system. The work established full-portfolio evidence, moved narrative translation into the engine contract, re-hardened execution and trajectory truth where product credibility required it, and shipped demo, proof, investor, analyst-validation, and pilot-handoff layers on top of curated engine artifacts.

## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- Full-portfolio proof had to land before stronger buyer, investor, or pilot storytelling could be trusted.
- The Python engine remained the source of truth, and website surfaces consumed curated typed artifacts rather than recomputing model logic.
- Behavior-aware and trajectory work were reopened only as targeted hardening passes, not as permission to redesign the v1.2 core.
- Default product surfaces had to read in business language, with technical detail available only through controlled reveal.
- Pilot-readiness claims were blocked until analyst UAT and explicit operator-baseline docs both existed.

**Issues Resolved:**
- Curated-demo storytelling is now anchored to full-cohort portfolio evidence and representativeness diagnostics.
- Demo, Proof, and Investor surfaces no longer read like raw artifact dashboards.
- Analyst-validation and pilot-handoff readiness now have milestone-local evidence rather than implied future work.
- The proof surface now consumes one curated product-confidence snapshot instead of stitching together many artifacts.

**Issues Deferred:**
- Pilot-grade external institution integrations remain deeper future work beyond curated artifact handoff.
- Regulatory explanation and adverse-action communication packs remain future milestone scope.
- Release review, proof refresh, and milestone health automation remain future work rather than shipped v1.3 capability.

**Technical Debt Incurred:**
- The local `gsd-tools.cjs` helper still cannot initialize `milestone-op`, so some milestone workflows remain manual.
- The website build still emits the existing main-bundle chunk-size warning, which stayed non-blocking through the shipped scope.
