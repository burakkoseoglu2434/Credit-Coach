# Milestone v2.2: Winner Integrity & Consumer Consistency

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-04-02
**Phases:** 45-48
**Total Plans:** 12

## Overview

This archive captures the milestone that repaired the remaining trust gaps after `v2.1`: invalid selected winners, DICE-lane contender weakness, selected-surface drift, and operator packet contradictions. The work made winner selection fail closed, enforced typed and semantic quality on `dice` contenders before selection, aligned raw and consumer-selected truth around one selected projection, and made operator packets decision-aware and internally coherent on the tracked blocker/action/readiness invariants.

## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- Winner integrity had to be fixed before any threshold widening or approval-rate optimization work.
- `dice` candidate generation could stay separate, but DICE action typing and contender gates had to converge onto the same canonical contract as internal plans.
- Selected decision truth had to be owned once and projected outward, rather than recomputed independently by downstream consumers.
- Operator packet coherence had to be measured from collection artifacts, not treated as a purely qualitative UI concern.

**Issues Resolved:**
- Invalid candidates can no longer leak back into winner selection through chooser fallback behavior.
- The `dice` lane no longer bypasses typed-action and contender-quality standards.
- Raw summary, flat metrics, operator packets, and explanation packs no longer drift on selected consumer truth.
- Approved packets no longer show fallback blockers or stray short-view actions on the tracked invariants.

**Issues Deferred:**
- Threshold widening and approval/conditional uplift tuning remain future work now that winner trust is clean.
- Offer-maturity and readiness semantics still need an outcome-optimization milestone rather than more trust repair.
- Naturally occurring `no_valid_winner` cohort evidence is still absent from large-sample reruns even though the empty-state contract is test-proven.

**Technical Debt Incurred:**
- Large-sample reruns still emit pandas `FutureWarning` messages from collection-frame concatenation in `run_customer_artifact_collection.py`.
- Local `gsd-tools.cjs` milestone-op helper still requires manual fallback because internal modules are missing.
