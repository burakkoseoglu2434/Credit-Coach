## 4. What Worked

- The trust baseline from `v2.2` remained clean through all bounded experiments.
- Phase 49 built a deterministic threshold-policy comparison layer and proved the repo can evaluate threshold-only candidates honestly.
- Phase 50 built deterministic offer/readiness outcome-rule evaluation and additive provenance without corrupting selected truth.
- Phase 51 identified the dominant bottleneck instead of hiding behind generic `no_safe_uplift` messaging.
- Phase 52 packaged the honest answer into collection and pilot-facing proof surfaces:
  - preferred demo profile = `preferred_demo_baseline_v2_3`
  - fallback profile = `trust_first_baseline_v2_3`
  - runtime baseline remains `v2.1_phase_40_5`
- The system now proves that it can reject weak optimization stories without breaking trust.

## 5. What Did NOT Work

- The milestone did not improve `APPROVED + CONDITIONAL` yield.
- No threshold-only candidate produced safe uplift.
- No offer/readiness-only outcome-rule candidate produced safe uplift.
- No candidate family created meaningful funnel movement in the three standard cohorts.
- The preferred policy baseline did not advance beyond the already-shipped runtime baseline.

## 6. Root Cause Analysis

The root cause is not a hidden regression. It is a capability limit.

The audited evidence says:
- bounded threshold changes were too weak to create safe lift
- bounded offer/readiness rules had no meaningful eligible population to upgrade
- the standard cohorts were overwhelmingly too far from near-term approval movement
- the current signal set and current action space are good enough to preserve correctness, but not strong enough to unlock business lift

`structural_cohort_limited` is the key finding. That means the bottleneck is not primarily "bad threshold tuning". It means too many customers in the audited cohorts are too far from a one-step or near-term move for those bounded levers to matter.

## 7. What Is Now Proven About The System

- The `v2.2` trust baseline is stable under bounded optimization experiments.
- The system can evaluate threshold and outcome-rule candidates without drifting selected truth or corrupting operator-facing artifacts.
- The system can publish explicit `no_safe_uplift` rather than implying promotion from weak comparisons.
- The honest preferred demo baseline is still `v2.1_phase_40_5`.

## 8. What Is NOT Yet Proven

- That Credit Coach can improve `APPROVED + CONDITIONAL` yield without expanding feature space or action semantics.
- That any bounded policy candidate should replace the shipped runtime baseline.
- That `structural_cohort_limited` would remain the dominant diagnosis under a broader action space, richer features, or a meaningfully different cohort mix.

## 9. Risks

- The biggest risk is narrative inflation: teams may describe `v2.3` as a successful optimization milestone because the engineering is clean. That would be false.
- Preferred/fallback profile labels could be mistaken for a newly promoted runtime policy. They are not.
- If the next milestone retries threshold-only or offer/readiness-only tuning again, it will likely burn time on a path that v2.3 already disproved.

## 10. Clear Recommendation For The Next Milestone

**Do not continue iterating on bounded threshold families or bounded offer/readiness-only rule families.**

That path has been tested and came back negative.

The next milestone should explicitly pivot to **movement capability expansion**, with a narrow and honest goal:
- expand the set of actionable movement levers
- preserve the `v2.2` trust contracts as non-negotiable
- test whether richer action semantics or richer movement signals create real near-term eligibility
- keep `v2.3` as the audited proof that the current bounded optimization surface is exhausted

Concrete next step:

1. Archive `v2.3` as a **truth-finding milestone, not a lift milestone**.
2. Open the next milestone around action-space or movement-lever expansion, with success defined as actual cohort movement, not just cleaner packaging.

## Final Judgment

`v2.3` was a useful milestone, but not a successful optimization milestone.

It succeeded at proving limits:
- the system remained correct
- the trust baseline held
- the bounded optimization thesis did not produce business lift

That is valuable. It is also not the same thing as solving the business problem.

**Bottom line:** keep the system, keep the trust baseline, keep the honest proof package, and stop trying to squeeze lift out of the current bounded policy surface. The milestone is ready for archival.
