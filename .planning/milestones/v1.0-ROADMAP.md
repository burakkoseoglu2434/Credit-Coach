## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- The project standardized on `.planning/` as the single planning home after importing legacy reports and plans.
- Release quality is judged by combined business KPI and governance gates rather than lift alone.
- Deterministic model state, trajectory metrics, and challenger evidence became part of the core release contract.

**Issues Resolved:**
- Core recourse generation now targets gate passing instead of technical candidate generation only.
- Telemetry export, release-gate reporting, and audit persistence exist in the codebase.
- High-risk and trajectory-specific evaluation paths were integrated into the platform.

**Issues Deferred:**
- Seed-stable fairness and full KPI repeatability remain open.
- Short-horizon trajectory success and candidate diversity still need additional hardening.
- Offline-to-online outcome loops are not yet mature enough for milestone closure.

**Technical Debt Incurred:**
- Large CLI orchestration modules remain on the hot path.
- Experiment config sprawl makes comparison and rollback harder than it should be.
