# Requirements: Credit Coach v1.3 Portfolio Intelligence & Productization

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-03-20
**Defined:** 2026-03-17
**Archived:** 2026-03-20
**Core Value:** Prove the recommendation engine across the full portfolio, translate its outputs into institution-ready product language, explicitly re-harden execution-aware and phased-roadmap behavior where needed, and turn the current website and demo surface into a trustworthy buyer, analyst, and pilot asset.

## v1.3 Requirements

### Portfolio Intelligence

- [x] **PORT-01**: The system can evaluate the full portfolio and report plan coverage, feasibility, execution-weighted improvement, offer reach, checkpoint health, and governance-safe rates across the whole evaluation population
- [x] **PORT-02**: The system can translate technical recommendation outputs into controlled analyst-friendly and buyer-friendly narratives with executive summaries by default and expandable technical traceability on demand
- [x] **PORT-03**: Behavior-aware recourse remains explicit, measurable, and ranking-relevant through action or plan execution probabilities, expected-utility ordering, execution scenarios, and supporting evidence artifacts
- [x] **PORT-04**: Sequential trajectory planning remains deterministic, checkpoint-governed, and product-ready through canonical phase assignment, fixed 4w or 12w or 24w checkpoints, soft monotonicity controls, stable trajectory statuses, and controlled replan semantics

### Website & Demo Productization

- [x] **WEB-01**: The website can consume curated demo and proof snapshots through a typed artifact-sync contract sourced from the engine
- [x] **WEB-02**: Demo Center and buyer-facing product surfaces can present single-customer, portfolio, offer, and learning views in business-first bilingual language
- [x] **WEB-03**: Proof and investor surfaces can explain measurement, governance, release controls, category story, and monetization logic without exposing raw internal artifact vocabulary

### Operational Validation

- [x] **OPS-01**: Analyst workflow and UAT can validate that outputs are understandable, useful, and trustworthy in representative institution tasks
- [x] **OPS-02**: Pilot-readiness baseline can support lead capture, deployment, auth or ops basics, and institution-specific curated demo flows

## Requirement Outcome Notes

- All 9 milestone requirements shipped and were verified by phase-level verification reports plus milestone audit.
- Phase 22 through Phase 25 explicitly closed the 2026-03-19 audit gaps on `WEB-01`, `WEB-02`, `WEB-03`, `OPS-01`, and `OPS-02`.
- The milestone preserved engine-first artifact truth, technical traceability on demand, and inherited fairness, reliability, telemetry, and release-governance constraints.

## Future Requirements Carried Forward

- **AUTO-01**: Release review, milestone health, and proof refreshes gain recurring automation support

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Rebuilding typed actions from scratch | Typed action realism shipped in `v1.2` and remains inherited baseline capability |
| Replacing behavior-aware ranking or trajectories with a brand-new design | `v1.3` revisited these layers as hardening work, but did not discard the shipped `v1.2` core |
| Full borrower self-serve app or mobile experience | This milestone stayed focused on enterprise buyer, analyst, and pilot surfaces |
| Fully online autonomous policy deployment | Learning remained evidence-first and non-blocking |
| Legal or compliance advice automation | The system remains an engineering and decision-support platform, not a legal advisor |
