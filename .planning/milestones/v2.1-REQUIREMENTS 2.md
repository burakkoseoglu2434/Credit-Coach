# Requirements: Credit Coach v2.1 Decision Ownership & Pilot-Ready Trust

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-03-23
**Defined:** 2026-03-23
**Archived:** 2026-03-23
**Core Value:** Transform the system from a decision-intelligence prototype into a deterministic, pilot-ready fintech decision system.

## v2.1 Requirements

### Contract And Trust

- [x] **TRST-01**: Single-customer and collection artifacts emit null-free decision-critical fields and checkpoint statuses across raw and flat outputs
- [x] **TRST-02**: Raw and flat customer decision artifacts stay parity-checked and classify contract faults without exposing `NEEDS_REVIEW` on business-facing surfaces

### Decision Ownership

- [x] **DEC-01**: Every customer receives deterministic internal and business-visible decision states using ordered policy logic, confidence gating, readiness gating, and zero-step rejection
- [x] **DEC-02**: Every non-rejected customer receives a one-line business explanation, structured numeric drivers, decision confidence, expected readiness, and upgrade condition
- [x] **DEC-03**: Offer maturity, conditional subtype, and rejection subtype truth explicitly determine whether a customer can be approved, conditional, or rejected

### Cohort And Calibration

- [x] **CAL-01**: Collection runs can evaluate decision funnels across mixed and stress-test cohorts with approve, conditional, rejected, and internal review shares
- [x] **CAL-02**: Decision thresholds can be tuned against cohort-level readiness, confidence-aware quality signals, false-positive proxy, and policy-version comparisons

### Operator Workflow

- [x] **OPS-01**: Operators can review 50 customers from one collection run in under 30 minutes using short decision packs with blockers, readiness, downside risk, and offer state
- [x] **OPS-02**: Collection summary and manifest expose pilot-review signals such as decision funnel, contract health, dominant rejection reasons, and conditional hot spots

### Planner Quality

- [x] **PLAN-01**: Best-plan selection eliminates zero-action winners, reports source mix, and improves internal planner competitiveness versus `dice`, `rule`, and `fallback` candidates

### Production Pilot Baseline

- [x] **PROD-01**: Deterministic decision-policy runs can be versioned, compared, and rerun through artifact QA and pilot refresh workflows
- [x] **PROD-02**: Institution-facing pilot docs and explanation packs can package deterministic decision policy without leaking internal-only states

## Requirement Outcome Notes

- All 12 milestone requirements shipped and passed both phase verification and the milestone audit.
- The milestone closed the gap between decision-intelligence artifacts and a deterministic, partner-reviewable fintech decision system.
- Customer-facing, operator-facing, calibration, planner-quality, and pilot-baseline artifacts all stayed consumers of the same owned decision truth rather than branching into separate logic layers.

## Future Requirements Carried Forward

### External Integrations

- **INTG-01**: Institution-specific inbound and outbound integrations can move from pilot packages to live system contracts
- **INTG-02**: Institution policy and offer configuration can synchronize continuously from external systems

### Governance And Automation

- **REG-01**: Adverse-action and regulatory explanation packs can expand into institution-ready compliance workflows
- **AUTO-01**: Proof refresh, release review, and milestone health checks can run as recurring automated workflows

## Out of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Borrower self-serve or mobile product expansion | `v2.1` focused on deterministic institution-facing decisions, not consumer channel expansion |
| Live online policy promotion | Decision ownership had to be hardened before autonomous policy rollout was considered |
| Full LOS, CRM, or call-center integration rollout | This milestone stopped at pilot-ready package and decision review baselines |
| Legal or compliance opinion automation | The system remains decision-support and explanation tooling, not legal advice software |
| Rebuilding the core recourse or website stacks from scratch | The milestone evolved the shipped brownfield contracts rather than replacing them |
