# Milestone v2.1: Decision Ownership & Pilot-Ready Trust

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-03-23
**Phases:** 40-44
**Total Plans:** 18

## Overview

This archive captures the milestone that turned Credit Coach from a decision-intelligence prototype into a deterministic, pilot-ready fintech decision system. The work repaired raw and flat artifact trust, established one owned decision policy with external three-state outcomes, added cohort-aware funnel and calibration evidence, packaged fast operator review surfaces, hardened planner provenance and source-mix honesty, and closed with a repeatable pilot baseline refresh, artifact QA, thin institution overlays, and institution-facing explanation packs.

## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- Decision ownership had to become an explicit producer-owned payload before cohort calibration, operator review, or pilot packaging could be trusted.
- Business-facing surfaces remained three-state consumers of owned decision truth rather than becoming alternative policy engines.
- Calibration stayed read-only and shadow-only, so measurement could improve without silently changing customer-visible decisions.
- Planner-quality evidence had to distinguish true planner wins from recovery-lane rescues to keep the pilot story honest.
- Production pilot readiness was framed as a controlled, rerunnable baseline rather than a live-integrated or autonomous decisioning claim.

**Issues Resolved:**
- Raw and flat artifacts no longer leak null decision fields, silent parity drift, or zero-step empty wins.
- The repo now has one deterministic decision owner instead of legacy `best_status` passthrough behavior.
- Collection runs can now describe the shipped decision system as a cohort-aware funnel with readiness and shadow calibration evidence.
- Operators can review customer outcomes through derived packets and briefings instead of opening raw technical artifacts by default.
- Pilot refreshes, explanation packs, and artifact QA now run through one repeatable baseline workflow.

**Issues Deferred:**
- Live institution integrations remain future work beyond local overlays and package-based pilot handoff.
- Compliance-adjacent explanation packs remain bounded support artifacts rather than full compliance automation.
- Proof refresh, release review, and milestone health automation remain future work beyond operator-triggered refresh workflows.

**Technical Debt Incurred:**
- The local `gsd-tools.cjs` helper still cannot initialize `milestone-op`, so some milestone workflows require manual fallback.
- Phase 41-44 validation frontmatter still understates `wave_0_complete` even though the dedicated test modules now exist and the milestone audit passed.
