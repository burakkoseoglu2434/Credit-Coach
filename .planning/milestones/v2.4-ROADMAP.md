# Milestone v2.4: Recourse Capability Expansion & Action Semantics

**Status:** ✅ SHIPPED 2026-04-05
**Phases:** 54-60
**Total Plans:** 19

## Overview

This archive captures the milestone that expanded Credit Coach beyond the bounded `v2.3` action surface without reopening the shipped `v2.2` trust contracts. The work established one canonical action-semantics layer, widened the DICE and institution/hybrid generation surface safely, organized valid contenders into deterministic alternative paths, rendered those paths coherently across customer and operator artifacts, and added one shared cohort-evidence layer for measuring movement potential and diversity. The milestone succeeded as a capability-expansion milestone, while explicitly not claiming business lift that has not yet been proven.

## Milestone Summary

**Key Decisions:**
- Expand action semantics and movement levers before retrying business-lift claims after `v2.3` proved the bounded policy surface was exhausted.
- Keep the `v2.2` trust baseline non-negotiable while widening the recourse surface.
- Treat DICE, institution/rule generation, pathing, rendering, and evaluation as additive layers over the validated candidate pool rather than planner redesigns.
- Treat higher path diversity as descriptive evidence only, not as automatic proof of business movement.

**Issues Resolved:**
- Action semantics are no longer split across ambiguous legacy literals.
- The system no longer depends only on customer/self-service style actions; institution-assisted and hybrid lanes now exist.
- Alternative-path evidence is no longer a flat candidate list; deterministic grouped-path artifacts now exist.
- Path and selected-action wording now come from one shared semantic/render seam instead of fragmented phrasing logic.
- Cohort-level evidence for path diversity, institution/hybrid rate, and selected-path distribution now exists in one reusable package.

**Issues Deferred:**
- Whether the richer v2.4 surface creates safe approval or conditional movement in real denied cohorts remains unproven.
- Whether `structural_cohort_limited` meaningfully softens under the expanded solution space remains a next-milestone question.
- Further action-taxonomy or planner-semantic expansion should wait until movement-outcome proof is gathered.

**Technical Debt Incurred:**
- Phase 54 still uses a legacy validation strategy document without Nyquist frontmatter.
- Phase 59 `VALIDATION.md` metadata still shows draft state despite a passed verification report.
- Local `gsd-tools.cjs` milestone-op helper still requires manual fallback because internal modules are missing.
