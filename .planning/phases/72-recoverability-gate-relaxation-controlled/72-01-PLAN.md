Update `_apply_recourse_gate_to_roadmap(...)` to implement Wave 1 controlled surfacing for assisted_full:

  - `pre_gate_threshold_reach_probability`

  - `pre_gate_selected_path_type`

  - exclude steps requiring institutional approval or acceptance
  - deterministically select the surfaced step (preference order documented and stable)
- prune roadmap phases + narrative to the single surfaced step

4) Fallback behavior:
- If eligible by signal but no allowlisted customer-only step is available, fall back to `limited` (no forced surfacing).

Guardrails:
- do not change best-plan selection, ranking, or candidate pool logic
- do not introduce multi-step surfacing or multi-action surfacing
