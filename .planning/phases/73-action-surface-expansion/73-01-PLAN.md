<objective>
Add top-k=2 surfaced actions for `full`-tier customers inside the recourse gate/projection layer of `run_recourse.py`. The second action is projected as new additive `selected_secondary_*` summary fields, never overwriting any action #1 field. Implements D-01 (fixed k=2), D-02 (diversity-first, keep winner unchanged), D-03/D-04 (full tier only; assisted_full/limited untouched).

Purpose: SURF-01 (expose more than one actionable candidate when multiple valid action families exist) and SURF-02 (diversity drawn only from existing internal action semantics), while preserving TRUST-04 (no narrative claims beyond selected-plan evidence) and TRUST-01/02/03 (trust counters stay at 0).

Output: Modified `run_recourse.py` with three new helper functions and an additive projection block; a new unit-test module proving the selection rules and the single-action fallback.
