# Credit Coach Website Plan

## Amaç

Bu website tek bir is yapmayacak. Uc farkli ihtiyaci ayni anda karsilayacak:

1. Yatirimciya projenin neden buyuk bir firsat oldugunu gosterecek
2. Potansiyel musterilere urunun ne ise yaradigini net anlatacak
3. Demo isteyen kisilere somut cikti, modul ve KPI gosterecek

Bu nedenle site yalnizca bir "landing page" olmamali. En dogru form:

- marketing site
- product explanation site
- interactive demo surface
- proof / evidence library

Kisa hedef cümlesi:

"Credit Coach, kredi kararini binary approve/deny cizgisinden alip, uygulanabilir recourse, kurumsal offer optimization ve learning loop ile yonetilen bir karar-istihbarati katmanina donusturur."

## 4. Onerdigim Sayfa Yapisi

## 4.1 Home

Amaç:

- ilk 20 saniyede projeyi anlasilir kilmak
- kullaniciyi dogru persona yoluna sokmak

Hero mesaj önerisi:

"Credit decisions should not end at deny."

Alt mesaj:

"Credit Coach helps lenders turn denied applications into feasible action plans, institution-side offers, and measurable recovery opportunities without breaking governance."

Hero altinda iki yol olmalı:

- `I’m a lender`
- `I’m an investor`

Home bolumleri:

1. Hero
2. Problem
3. What Credit Coach Does
4. Key Outcomes
5. Module Strip
6. Demo Preview
7. Governance / Safety
8. Investor Snapshot
9. Final CTA

Home’da gosterecegimiz en guclu 6 sinyal:

- denied to approved uplift
- average PD reduction
- mean plan execution probability
- 4-week trajectory gate pass
- offer acceptance probability mean
- decision log completeness / reward coverage

## 4.2 Product

Amaç:

- urunu sade ama ciddi bir sekilde anlatmak

Ana bolumler:

1. From deny to guided improvement
2. How the decision engine works
3. How the recommendation engine works
4. How governance stays in the loop
5. How outcomes feed learning

Burada teknik jargon azaltilmali.

Anlatim dili su olmali:

- detect risk
- explain causes
- generate feasible plans
- test governance
- optimize offers
- learn from outcomes

## 4.3 Modules

- Why it matters
